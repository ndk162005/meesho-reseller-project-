Zero-Order Resellers & LEFT JOIN Nuance Demo

Demonstrates why COUNT(*) returns 1 for unmatched rows while COUNT(order_id) correctly yields 0.

Outputs: zero_order_resellers.csv & zero_order_count_demo.csv
SQL Insight: When performing resellers LEFT JOIN orders, reseller RS024 has no matching order rows. A COUNT(*) evaluates the presence of the single outer-joined row and yields 1. In contrast, COUNT(o.order_id) inspects the nullable foreign key and correctly yields 0.
