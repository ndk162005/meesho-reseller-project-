# Meesho Reseller Intelligence — Prompt Pack & Narrative Rules

## 1. Core Framework
All business narratives generated by the intelligence system must follow the three-tier analytical structure:

1. **Context**: Background figures, comparison period, and baseline revenue numbers directly from verified SQL output.
2. **Insight**: Exact numerical Month-over-Month (MoM) calculation and growth direction.
3. **Implication**: Actionable business hypothesis clearly labeled as a hypothesis (not a proven fact).

---

## 2. Distinction Between Facts and Hypotheses

### Facts (Strictly Data-Backed)
* Must reflect numbers produced by `monthly_category_revenue.csv` or `growth_engine.py`.
* Example: *"Ethnic Wear revenue increased by +77.10% in May compared to April."*

### Hypotheses (Interpretation / Business Context)
* Explanations for observed data that cannot be proven directly from raw sales tables alone.
* MUST use speculative framing: *"This may indicate...", "A potential driver could be...", "Hypothesis:"*.
* Example: *"Hypothesis: Seasonal demand spikes for festive ethnic apparel during May may have driven higher conversion."*

---

## 3. Privacy & Masking Directive
* Raw reseller personal names must NEVER appear in narrative output.
* All reseller references must pass through `part3_narrative.masking` to produce deterministic identifiers (e.g., `Reseller R001`).
